Square Digital KDM Viewer shows you what's inside a Key Delivery Message at a glance: which film it unlocks, when the key opens and expires, and which server or sound processor it's made for.

Opening KDMs

  • Mac: drop a KDM or a zip of KDMs onto the window, use Browse, or right-click a file and choose Open With › KDM Viewer.
  • iPhone and iPad: tap Browse Files, or tap a KDM or zip attachment in Mail, tap Share, and choose KDM Viewer.
  • Zips with several KDMs: use the arrows at the top to step through them, or tap "KDM 1 of 5" to jump to a specific screen.

Common questions

Which KDMs does it read?

Standard SMPTE and Interop KDMs in .xml format, the kind distributors send for digital cinema servers, and zips of them. Password-protected zips need to be unzipped first.

Why doesn't it recognize my server or processor model?

Manufacturers name their certificates differently. If your device shows as not recognized, open More info, copy the recipient subject and certificate issuer lines, and email them to us so we can add it.

The film details look wrong or are missing.

These come from the DCP name. If a distributor doesn't follow the usual naming convention, some details can't be read. The key times and device details aren't affected.

Does it change or send my KDMs anywhere?

No. It only reads the file you open, on your device. See the privacy policy.
