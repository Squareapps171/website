KDM Viewer privacy policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Square Digital KDM Viewer is designed to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information we collect

None. The app doesn't collect, store, or send any personal information to Square Digital or anyone else. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Files you open

When you open a KDM or a zip of KDMs, the app reads it on your device to show you its contents. Files are never uploaded, copied elsewhere, or changed. The app doesn't connect to the internet at all.

Files you save or share

If you save, share, or print a PDF report, it goes only where you choose.

Settings

The app remembers your chosen time zone. That setting is stored on your device and never leaves it.

Children's privacy

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email apps@square-digital.com.
